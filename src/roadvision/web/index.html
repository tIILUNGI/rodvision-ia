7 Dimensions of Visual Reasoning in Multimodal AI

Most vision-language models hallucinate or fail spatial tests. RoadVision AI systematically isolates each cognitive failure mode through deterministic ground truth.

Defect Counting & Density

Tests whether the AI correctly counts multiple distinct potholes and cracks without double-counting adjacent anomalies.

Severity Classification

Distinguishes structural craters from superficial surface wear, alligator cracking, and pre-existing bitumen patches.

Bounding Box Localization

Evaluates normalized coordinates [ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax] scored against exact pixel-contour IoU thresholds.

Spatial Topology Reasoning

Verifies relative spatial orientation: determining if a defect lies left, right, foreground, or behind other road features.

Area & Metric Comparison

Compares surface footprint between competing anomalies in projected real-world square meters, not just 2D pixel size.

Occlusion & Weather Robustness

Stress-tests vision models when defects are partially hidden under tree shadows, heavy rain reflections, or road debris.

Evidence Sufficiency & Abstention

Rewards models that explicitly declare "insufficient visual evidence" instead of fabricating answers under low visibility.
